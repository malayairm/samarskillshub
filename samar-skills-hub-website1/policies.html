Enrollment and payment

How to enroll

  1. Complete the online enrollment form. The name you enter will appear on your certificate.
  2. Pay the full course fee through an official Samar Skills Hub account only. Payment details are shown on the enrollment form and in the instructions we send you.
  3. Upload your proof of payment with the reference Batch##-Surname.
  4. Your seat is confirmed only after we verify your payment in our account. We reply within 24 hours.

No-refund and reschedule policy

  • Course fees are non-refundable once your payment is verified.
  • You may reschedule once to a future batch. Ask at least 3 days before Day 1 and use your new seat within 3 months.
  • After Day 1 starts, rescheduling is not allowed. The missed-session rule below still applies.
  • If Samar Skills Hub moves or cancels a batch, for example because it doesn't reach 25 students, you are moved to the next batch at no cost and keep the price you paid.
  • To request a reschedule, message us with your name, current batch and preferred new batch.
  • Rescheduling is not available to students removed for breaking the Code of Conduct or the Recording and Content Use Policy.

Attendance and completion

  • Join each live Zoom session on time, preferably on a laptop or desktop, using your enrolled name.
  • You may miss one live session and still complete if you watch its recording and submit that day's homework within 3 days.
  • If you miss two or more sessions, you won't complete that batch, but you may attend the missed sessions in the next batch once, free of charge.

To complete the course

  • Meet the attendance rule.
  • Submit at least 3 of 4 homework tasks.
  • Score at least 75% on the capstone, due 3 days after Day 5.

If you score 60–74% on the capstone, you may resubmit once within 3 days of getting your result. If a session is interrupted by a problem on our side, we continue, reschedule within 48 hours, or provide a recording, and it won't count as your absence.

Privacy notice

Samar Skills Hub processes personal data in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect and why

  • Name, email, mobile and Facebook profile: enrollment, communication, Group access and certificates.
  • Referrer's name: applying referral discounts.
  • Location, current work and experience level: understanding our students and improving courses.
  • Payment amount, reference and proof of payment: verifying payment and keeping records.
  • Attendance, homework and capstone scores: tracking progress and deciding completion.
  • Your image and voice in live recordings: letting classmates review lessons.
  • Feedback and testimonials: improving courses, and marketing only with your separate permission.

How we protect and share it

  • Data is stored in Samar Skills Hub's Google Drive and Sheets, accessible only to authorized staff for their role.
  • We never post contact details, payments or grades in the Facebook Group, and we do not sell your data.
  • We share data only with the services we use to run courses (Google, Zoom, Meta and payment providers) or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Enrollment, payment and certificate records: 5 years.
  • Recordings of your live batch: available for 60 days after Day 5, then deleted.
  • Homework and capstone files: 1 year.
  • Inquiries from people who did not enroll: 1 year, or sooner on request.

Your rights

You have the right to be informed, to access your data, to object to processing, to have errors corrected, to request erasure or blocking, to data portability, to claim damages, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To use these rights, email samarskillshub@gmail.com. We respond within 15 days.

Recording and content use

  • Live sessions are recorded for the students of that batch. If you prefer not to appear, keep your camera off and use the chat.
  • Students get lifetime access to the course master recordings, a clean set of lessons showing only the trainer's screen, for as long as Samar Skills Hub offers the course.
  • Students may not record sessions, screenshot other students, or share, upload or sell any course materials, recordings or Group content.
  • Breaking this policy may lead to removal from the course and Group, with no refund, reschedule or certificate.
  • You keep ownership of the homework and capstone work you submit. We use it as an anonymous teaching example only with your permission.

Community code of conduct

This applies to Zoom sessions, Messenger and our private Facebook Group.

  • Be respectful and supportive toward students, trainers and staff, and use your real name.
  • Not allowed: harassment, discrimination or offensive content; selling or recruiting without permission; posting anyone's personal data or real client data; sharing course materials outside the Group; sharing unverified job posts or schemes that ask for payment.
  • First issue: a private reminder. Second or serious issue: post removed and a written warning. Repeated or severe issues: removal from the Group and course, with no refund or reschedule.
