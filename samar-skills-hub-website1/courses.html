Common questions

Before you enroll.

Still unsure? Message us on Facebook and we'll reply within 24 hours, Monday to Saturday.

Message us
Do I need an accounting background?

No. The bootcamp starts from the basics and builds up with guided practice.

What do I need to join?

A laptop or desktop, a stable internet connection from 6:00 to 9:00 PM, and a free Xero account and QuickBooks test drive, which we help you set up before Day 1.

What if I miss a class?

You can miss one live session and still complete the course if you watch its recording and submit that day's homework within three days.

Can I join on my phone?

You can follow the live Zoom sessions on a phone, but we recommend a laptop or desktop. The hands-on practice in Xero and QuickBooks works best on a computer.

What if my internet drops during class?

Rejoin as soon as you can. If you miss a whole session, you can still complete the course by watching its recording and submitting that day's homework within 3 days. If a session is interrupted by a problem on our side, we continue, reschedule within 48 hours, or provide a recording, and it won't count as your absence.

How long can I watch the recordings?

There are two kinds. The recordings of your own live batch are available for 60 days after Day 5, then deleted. The course master recordings, a clean set of lessons showing only the trainer's screen, are yours for life, for as long as Samar Skills Hub offers the course.

What do I need to complete the course?

Meet the attendance rule, submit at least 3 of 4 homework tasks, and score at least 75% on the capstone, due 3 days after Day 5. You then receive a Certificate of Completion from Samar Skills Hub, with the name you entered on the enrollment form.

When is the next batch?

Batch 02 runs November 23–27, 2026 and Batch 03 runs December 14–18, 2026. See all upcoming batches.

Is this a TESDA course or an official certification?

No. You receive a Certificate of Completion from Samar Skills Hub. It is not a TESDA National Certificate and not a certification from Xero or Intuit.

Will I get a job after the course?

We can't guarantee work, clients or income. We teach the skills and share practical tips for finding clients.

Can I get a refund?

Course fees are non-refundable once your payment is verified, but you may reschedule once to a future batch if you ask at least 3 days before Day 1 and use your new seat within 3 months.

How many students are in a batch?

Each batch runs with 25 to 50 students. If a batch doesn't reach 25, we move it to a new date and you keep your seat and price.
