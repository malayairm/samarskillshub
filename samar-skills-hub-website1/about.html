About Samar Skills Hub.

We teach practical digital skills in live evening classes, so Filipinos can learn without giving up their day jobs.

Our story

Hands-on digital training, closer to home.

Samar Skills Hub was built to bring hands-on digital training closer to home.

Many people in Samar want to work remotely or grow in their current jobs, but most training is expensive, too theoretical, or held during office hours.

We offer practical courses in the digital skills clients and employers need, taught live on Zoom from 6:00 to 9:00 PM so you can learn after work. We're starting with bookkeeping in QuickBooks and Xero, with more courses on the way, including accounts payable and receivable, payroll support, Canva, video editing, social media management and AI tools.

Every lesson is hands-on, every session is recorded, and every student can ask questions as they learn.

Our mission

To give Filipinos practical, job-ready digital skills through live, affordable, evening training.

Our vision

To grow from online short courses into a trusted training center, and in time, a school that serves Samar and the wider region.
