Bringing training closer to Samar.

We're working with partners to bring free or low-cost digital skills training closer to learners in Samar and the Visayas. This page shows where our discussions stand.

Last updated November 3, 2026

Where things stand

In discussion

TESDA Northern Samar

We have presented a proposal to TESDA Northern Samar to offer TESDA-registered programs through Samar Skills Hub. Samar Skills Hub is not yet TESDA-accredited; we will announce any update here once it is official.

Seeking partners

Foundations and sponsors

We are reaching out to foundations and sponsors who can fund free or sponsored training slots for qualified learners in Samar and the Visayas.

Seeking partners

Call centers and employers

We are talking with employers about interviewing our graduates after they complete their training.

Open

LGUs, schools and organizations

Groups of 10 or more learners in the same batch can train at our partner rate, for livelihood programs, employee upskilling and school partnerships.

Good news, without the guesswork.
